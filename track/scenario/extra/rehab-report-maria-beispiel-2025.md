# Reha Waldblick: discharge report

Patient: Maria Beispiel, born 12 March 1961
Stay: 13 October to 7 November 2025
Reason: conservative rehabilitation for osteoarthritis of the left hip

## Course

On arrival, Maria had pain in the left hip at rest and walked 200 metres with one crutch. With physiotherapy, hydrotherapy and weight loss counselling, she now walks 400 metres with one crutch. Pain at rest went down from 6 to 3 of 10. Range of motion in the left hip stays limited. There were no falls.

## Medication at discharge

- Paracetamol 1000 mg up to three times a day if needed
- Diclofenac gel on the left hip twice a day

## Recommendations

- Continue the home exercises.
- Outpatient physiotherapy once a week.
- Review at the orthopaedic outpatient clinic of Klinikum Beispielstadt about a hip replacement in spring 2026.
