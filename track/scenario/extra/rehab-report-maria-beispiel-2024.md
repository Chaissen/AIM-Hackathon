# Reha Waldblick: discharge report

Patient: Maria Beispiel, born 12 March 1961
Stay: 28 December to 25 January 2024
Reason: rehabilitation after a fracture of the right ankle (lateral malleolus), operated with a plate at Klinikum Beispielstadt on 14 December 2023

## Course

On arrival, Maria was allowed to put partial weight on the right foot and walked with two crutches. Full weight bearing was allowed from 10 January 2024. At discharge she walks without aids on level ground and uses one crutch outdoors. The wound healed without problems.
Pain at discharge: 2 of 10, only after long walks.

## Medication at discharge

- Paracetamol 500 mg up to three times a day if needed

## Recommendations

- Remove the plate at Klinikum Beispielstadt no earlier than 12 months after the operation, only if it causes problems.
- Outpatient physiotherapy twice a week for four weeks.
